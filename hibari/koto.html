Koto

The first model of the Hibari engine — current release Hibari-Koto1.3.1:29M

Koto is a small, friendly chat model for short conversations, empathy and simple writing help. Its repository holds everything model-specific (configs, tokenizer, data recipes, training and evaluation code, experiment records, release files) and is checked out inside Hibari as the submodule models/Koto.

Honest summary. 1.3.1 follows conversations better than 1.1.1, handles rude or odd input more calmly and keeps more context, and it is clearly better than 1.3.0 everywhere. It is worse at rewriting (rephrase, make formal) and at writing e-mails, and it did not meet the project's own acceptance rule for the behaviour suite (beat 0.660 with rewriting ≥ 0.67). It was released for its conversational gains, with this regression stated in every card. Pass rates are a pass/fail measure of specific expectations, not a measure of understanding.

Limitations

Read before use
  • Small model, 256-token context: it forgets early turns of long chats and cannot write long texts.
  • It invents facts; it is not a source for factual, medical, legal or financial answers.
  • Rewriting and e-mail writing are weak in this version.
  • In Ollama and llama.cpp there is no safety layer; it is not a crisis service.
  • English only.
